# CSDA Data-Search Agent 🛰️

Find and download NASA **CSDA** (Commercial Smallsat Data Acquisition)
Earth-observation data by chatting in natural language.

**What it can do**
- Discover available datasets (STAC collections)
- Search items by area (bounding box) and date range
- Download assets using your Earthdata Login credentials

**Before downloading:** open **Settings** (⚙️ icon) and enter your
[Earthdata Login](https://urs.earthdata.nasa.gov) username and password.
They are kept in your session only and never stored.
